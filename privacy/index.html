Postool uses a Cloudflare backend to store product records and connected platform credentials. Videos are selected from your device or Google Drive; some publishing flows temporarily place a local video in your Google Drive.

1. Overview

This policy explains how Postool processes information when you visit the public website or use its video publishing features. Postool uses Google sign-in, Cloudflare backend services, and connected social platform APIs to provide publishing workflows.

2. Information we process

Google account and media data

When you sign in with Google, Postool processes basic account information supplied by Google. If you connect Google Drive, Postool processes the identifiers, names, types, sizes, thumbnails, and access information needed to select, upload, read, publish, or clean up video files. TikTok scheduling and Instagram publishing may upload a local video to the __POSTOOL__/Videos folder in your Drive.

Connected platform data

When you connect TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, or Instagram, Postool processes OAuth authorization codes, access and refresh tokens, scopes, profile identifiers, display names, avatars, privacy options, and publishing eligibility information returned by that platform.

Content and publishing data

We process brands, posts, platform-specific post records, videos, media URLs, captions, titles, descriptions, selected destinations, status responses, platform post identifiers, published URLs, and error details needed to perform and report the action you request.

Technical information

GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Google, and connected platforms may process standard request data such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and security logs under their own policies. Postool does not currently operate an advertising or cross-site tracking system.

3. How we use information

Postool processes information to:

  • authenticate you and connect accounts you select;
  • create and maintain your Postool workspace;
  • display the correct platform destination before you publish;
  • exchange, store, and refresh credentials when required for publishing;
  • deliver media, initiate publication, poll status, and record results;
  • run scheduled publishing jobs when scheduling is enabled;
  • protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and comply with law and platform rules; and
  • communicate material service or policy changes through the product.

Postool does not sell personal information and does not use your videos, captions, or platform credentials for advertising.

4. Where information is stored

Postool backend

Postool stores operational product records in Cloudflare services, including brands, platform accounts, posts, publishing history, settings, access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiration times, platform metadata, and small compressed video thumbnails used in post lists. Access to these records is restricted through authenticated backend endpoints and user ownership checks.

Media delivery

Postool does not use its main database as permanent video storage. When a publishing flow requires a remotely accessible file, Postool may upload a local video to your Google Drive and may temporarily relay an allowed media source through a signed Worker URL. Files created by Postool can be marked for later cleanup. Network infrastructure may transiently buffer request data during transfer.

Browser storage

Browser storage may cache short-lived login state, a selected local video needed to complete publishing, and app preferences such as theme or locale. Clearing browser site data removes this local cache and may prevent an unfinished local upload from resuming without selecting the file again.

5. When information is shared

Information is transmitted only as needed to providers involved in your request:

  • Google, for sign-in and optional Drive media access;
  • TikTok and other platforms you connect, for authorization, account confirmation, media publishing, and status;
  • Cloudflare, for backend execution, database storage, security, and media delivery; and
  • GitHub, which hosts the static public website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a reorganization where this policy continues to apply. We do not provide user data to data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Postool keeps product records and connected credentials for as long as needed to provide the service. Disconnecting a social platform clears its stored credentials. Revoked or invalid Google Drive credentials are removed when Postool detects that Drive access is no longer valid. Deleting a brand removes its connected platform accounts and cancels associated unfinished work; published history may remain.

Postool does not currently provide a delete-post control. To remove content already published, use the destination platform's deletion controls. For account deletion, data deletion, or export assistance, contact us as described below.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information.

You may also:

  • decline authorization, although related features will not work;
  • revoke Google access from your Google Account security settings;
  • revoke TikTok or other platform access from that platform's settings;
  • clear Postool browser storage; and
  • submit a privacy request through the contact channel below.

8. Security

Postool uses HTTPS, OAuth, origin and redirect allowlists, backend authorization checks, signed media URLs, and limited-purpose Worker endpoints. No system is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your Google and platform accounts. Do not share tokens or authorization codes.

9. Children

Postool is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International processing and changes

Providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable legal safeguards. We may update this policy as features or providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version, and material changes will be presented through the site or product where practical.

11. Contact and privacy requests

Submit privacy questions, deletion requests, or export requests through the Postool GitHub issue tracker. Do not include access tokens, authorization codes, private videos, or other sensitive data in a public issue.
