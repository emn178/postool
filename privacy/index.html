Privacy Policy

Effective August 13, 2026

In the review build, Postool stores product records and platform credentials in a Google Sheet in your Google account. Postool's token broker processes credentials during exchange or refresh but does not persist them.

1. Overview

This policy explains how Postool processes information when you visit the public website or use its video publishing features. Postool is designed to minimize central data custody by using Google resources owned by the user wherever practical.

2. Information we process

Google account and Drive data

When you sign in with Google, Postool processes basic account information supplied by Google and uses authorized Google APIs to discover, create, validate, and update the __POSTOOL__ folder and spreadsheet in your Drive. The Sheet can contain brands, platform accounts, posts, publishing history, settings, access tokens, refresh tokens, token expiration times, and platform metadata.

Connected platform data

When you connect a platform such as TikTok, Postool processes OAuth authorization codes, access and refresh tokens, scopes, profile identifiers, display names, avatars, privacy options, and publishing eligibility information returned by that platform.

Content and publishing data

We process the videos, media URLs, captions, titles, descriptions, selected destinations, status responses, platform post identifiers, published URLs, and error details needed to perform and report the action you request.

Technical information

GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Google, and connected platforms may process standard request data such as IP address, user agent, timestamps, and security logs under their own policies. Postool does not currently operate an advertising or cross-site tracking system.

3. How we use information

Postool processes information to:

  • authenticate you and connect accounts you select;
  • find or create your user-owned Postool data store;
  • display the correct platform destination before you publish;
  • exchange or refresh credentials when an app secret cannot safely run in the browser;
  • deliver media, initiate publication, poll status, and record results;
  • protect the service, troubleshoot errors, and comply with law and platform rules; and
  • communicate material service or policy changes through the product.

Postool does not sell personal information and does not use your videos, captions, or platform credentials for advertising.

4. Where information is stored

Your Google account

The review build stores product records—including platform credentials—in the __POSTOOL__ Google Sheet in your account. Browser storage may cache the spreadsheet identifier and short-lived Google access information to reduce repeated setup; the Google Drive discovery flow remains the source of truth.

Postool Worker

A Cloudflare Worker handles operations that require confidential platform app credentials, including OAuth token exchange or refresh. It returns the result to the requesting flow and is designed not to persist user access tokens, refresh tokens, Google Sheet identifiers, post content, or media files.

Media delivery

When requested for publishing, a Postool Worker may temporarily relay an allowed media source so a platform can fetch the video from a verified Postool URL. Network infrastructure may transiently buffer request data, but Postool does not use the relay as permanent media storage. A future release may use short-lived Cloudflare R2 objects and signed URLs; this policy will be updated before that behavior is introduced.

5. When information is shared

Information is transmitted only as needed to providers involved in your request:

  • Google, for sign-in, Drive, and Sheets operations;
  • TikTok and other platforms you connect, for authorization, account confirmation, media publishing, and status;
  • Cloudflare, for Worker execution, security, and media delivery; and
  • GitHub, which hosts the static public website.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a reorganization where this policy continues to apply. We do not provide user data to data brokers.

6. Retention and deletion

Records in your Google Sheet remain there until you edit or delete them. You can delete individual Postool records or remove the __POSTOOL__ folder and spreadsheet from Google Drive. Clearing browser site data removes locally cached identifiers and tokens. Revoking Google or platform access prevents future authorized API use but may not delete records already in your Google account or posts already published to a platform.

To remove a published post, use the destination platform's deletion controls. To disconnect credentials, revoke Postool in the provider's connected-app settings and remove the corresponding PlatformAccounts row from your Sheet. For help with deletion, contact us as described below.

7. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or obtain a copy of personal information. Because most Postool records are stored in resources you own, you can inspect, export, edit, or delete them directly in Google Sheets.

You may also:

  • decline authorization, although related features will not work;
  • revoke Google access from your Google Account security settings;
  • revoke TikTok or other platform access from that platform's settings;
  • clear Postool browser storage; and
  • submit a privacy request through the contact channel below.

8. Security

Postool uses HTTPS, OAuth, origin and redirect allowlists, signed state where supported, and limited-purpose Worker endpoints. No system is completely secure, and you are responsible for protecting your Google and platform accounts. Do not share tokens or authorization codes.

9. Children

Postool is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum age required in their jurisdiction, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. International processing and changes

Providers may process information in countries other than your own under their applicable legal safeguards. We may update this policy as features or providers change. The effective date above identifies the current version, and material changes will be presented through the site or product where practical.

11. Contact and privacy requests

Submit privacy questions or deletion requests through the Postool GitHub issue tracker. Do not include access tokens, authorization codes, private videos, or other sensitive data in a public issue.
